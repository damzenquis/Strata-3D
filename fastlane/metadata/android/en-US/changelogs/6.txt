* First release as Strata:3D, a new app with its own identity
* New formats: PLY (ASCII and binary), 3MF and glTF/GLB
* New look: Strata:3D logo and icon, light/dark toggle that is remembered, settings side panel
* 3MF: multi-part objects (components) and Bambu Studio / Orca Slicer project files now open
* PLY: fixed binary little-endian files that use 16-bit values
* glTF: Draco, meshopt and quantized compression, sparse accessors, and glTFs with a separate .bin (pick the files together, or open a .zip)
