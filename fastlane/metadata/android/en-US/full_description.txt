Strata:3D is a fast, lightweight, open-source 3D model viewer for Android. No ads, no trackers, and no internet permission.

<b>Formats</b>
* STL (binary and ASCII), including CAD exports with unusual headers
* Wavefront OBJ: triangles, quads and n-gons
* PLY: ASCII and binary, as written by 3D scanners
* 3MF: including multi-part objects and Bambu Studio / Orca Slicer project files
* glTF and GLB: including Draco, meshopt and quantized compression
* glTFs with a separate .bin file: select the files together, or open a .zip

<b>Viewing</b>
* Smooth OpenGL ES 3.0 rendering of multi-million-triangle models with 4x anti-aliasing
* Solid, Solid + edges and Wireframe modes
* Drag to orbit, two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset
* Build-plate grid that adapts to the model's size
* Choice of model colours
* Perspective or orthographic view, with one-tap Top, Front, Side and Iso views
* Portrait and landscape layouts

<b>Inspecting</b>
* Measure the distance between two points, with ΔX/ΔY/ΔZ, in real units, with snapping to visible vertices
* Section: cut the model along X, Y or Z to see inside; closed models get a filled cut that shows wall thickness
* Mesh check: find open edges, non-manifold edges and flipped or inside-out parts, highlighted in 3D
* Model info: triangle count, dimensions in mm, volume, surface area, file size and load time
* File location, and saving a copy as a new file

<b>Everyday use</b>
* Snapshot & Share: transparent or framed PNG images up to 4K
* Recent Files: reopen your last models in one tap
* Open models from the file picker, "Open with" in any file manager, or the Share sheet
* Light and dark themes with a one-tap toggle that remembers your choice
